# Riderly Motorbike Rentals

Make the trip unforgettable on two wheels. Riderly connects you with local motorbike and
scooter rental shops, so you can find the right bike for your destination and dates
without leaving the conversation.

Tell Claude where and when you are traveling and get live availability with daily prices
and an estimated total in your preferred currency. Compare scooters, motorcycles, and quads
side by side, check shop ratings from verified reviews, and see what each rental includes
before you commit: licence category, minimum rider age and experience, security deposit,
mileage, and whether you can cross borders.

Found your ride? Open it on Riderly to complete your booking.

## Try asking

- "Find a scooter in Lisbon from July 10 to July 14."
- "Show motorcycles in Barcelona for next week, with prices in GBP."
- "Compare the deposits and licence requirements for these bikes."

## Good to know

- Prices are estimates. Final price, availability, and booking terms are confirmed on
  Riderly before you pay.
- Booking and payment happen on riderly.com, not in chat. The plugin cannot change or
  cancel existing bookings.
- No Riderly account is needed to search. The plugin connects to Riderly's server at
  https://mcp.riderly.com/mcp. Riderly records search details (destination, dates,
  filters) and listing clicks to improve the service. See the
  [privacy policy](https://riderly.com/privacy-policy/).
- Need help? [Contact Riderly](https://riderly.com/contact-us/).
